2. What ComplyKit does and does not do

ComplyKit checks web pages against automated rules based on WCAG 2.2 level AA, using the open-source axe-core engine. It marks issues on the page, explains them, maps them to WCAG and EN 301 549 criteria, exports PDF reports and creates text prompts you can paste into AI tools.

ComplyKit does not:

  • give legal advice or create a lawyer–client relationship;
  • certify, guarantee or determine the legal compliance of any website;
  • find every accessibility issue (many need manual review with a keyboard, screen readers and real users);
  • protect you or your clients from complaints, demand letters, lawsuits or regulatory action.

A score, a report with no issues or any other result does not mean a site is accessible or compliant. Fix suggestions and AI prompts may be wrong or incomplete; review and test every change before you use it.
